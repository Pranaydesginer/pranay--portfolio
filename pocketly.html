The idea.

money, made simple.

Overview

Pocketly is a money management app that helps Gen Z users save. It tracks where they spend their money, daily and weekly, and gives suggestions on how to change their spending.

What users can do

  • See income and spending in one place.
  • Track spending daily and weekly, by category.
  • Get suggestions on how to change their money usage.
  • Open any transaction to see its details, add notes, edit or delete it.

Design decisions.

small choices, clear money.

Colour tells the story

Income shows in green; spending shows in red with a minus sign, so the direction of money is clear before reading a word.

One tag, two places

The category sits as a soft pill under the merchant and again in Details, so the same label is always in the same style.

Merchants you recognise

Each transaction leads with the merchant’s logo in a tile, which is faster to scan than text alone.

Payment at a glance

An icon next to the name shows how it was paid: account, UPI, Phone Pe or credit and debit card.

Notes for context

A short note answers “what was this for?”, like “Cab to return home” or “Coffee after work”.

Safe actions

Edit is a calm, full-width button; Delete is red text set apart below it, so it is hard to tap by mistake.

Money in, money out.

green in, red out.

Categories and ways to pay.

Each transaction carries its category, such as Entertainment, Food & Dining, Transport or Income, and how it was paid: account, UPI, Phone Pe or credit and debit card.
