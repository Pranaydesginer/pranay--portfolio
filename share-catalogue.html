The idea.

one link, the whole stock.

Overview

Share Catalogue lets a jeweller share products that exist in stock through a URL. The customer opens the catalogue link, sees the available products, and chooses the ones they like.

What the customer can do

  • Browse in-stock pieces with barcode, metal, purity, weights and price.
  • Filter by category, metal, purity, price range, diamond carat weight and weight.
  • Save pieces to a wishlist and set quantities.
  • Ask for a piece: Buy now, Visit store or Make an order.

The problem.

where it was breaking.

Retailers relied on manual product sharing, making it difficult for customers to discover jewellery remotely while offering limited control over shared information.

01

Manual sharing

Retailers had to share their catalogue manually. There was no proper way to share it through the web, which made for a poor customer experience.

02

Limited control

Retailers had limited control over the product information they shared.

03

No proper filtering

Customers had to type from–to ranges into input fields, and there was no sidebar filtration.

Before

  • Filtering meant typing a from–to range into input fields.
  • No proper filter options for jewellery attributes.
  • No sidebar filters, so filtering was hard to find and use.

After

  • A sidebar filter panel that stays in view while browsing.
  • Tap-to-select chips for category, metal and purity.
  • Range sliders for price, diamond carat weight and weight, with quick-select presets.
  • Applied filters shown as removable chips, with Clear all and Reset.

How I approached it.

research first, pixels later.

Desk research

Read articles on catalogue and e-commerce filtering to understand how people narrow down large product lists.

ChatGPT

Used to explore and compare filter patterns and structure ideas before designing.

Figma

Wireframes, components and high-fidelity screens for web, laptop and mobile.

Key screens.

Find the right piece, fast

A filter panel keeps every jewellery attribute in one place: category, metal, purity, price range, diamond carat weight with quick-select ranges, and gross or net weight. Applied filters show as removable chips, with Clear all and Reset.

Everything about a piece, in one view

The product view shows the photos with a carousel, barcode, metal and purity, gross and net weight, HUID, size, stones and the price inclusive of taxes. The customer picks how they want the item, adds their name and phone, and submits a request.

A wishlist that is ready to request

Saved pieces sit in a wishlist with category tabs, quantity controls and an item count. Request all sends the whole list in one go. Products without photos still show clearly with an Image unavailable state.
