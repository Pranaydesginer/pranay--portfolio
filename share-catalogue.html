The idea.

one link, the whole stock.

Overview

Share Catalogue lets a jeweller share products that exist in stock through a URL. The customer opens the catalogue link, sees the available products, and chooses the ones they like.

What the customer can do

  • Browse in-stock pieces with barcode, metal, purity, weights and price.
  • Filter by category, metal, purity, price range, diamond carat weight and weight.
  • Save pieces to a wishlist and set quantities.
  • Ask for a piece: Buy now, Visit store or Make an order.

Key screens.

Find the right piece, fast

A filter panel keeps every jewellery attribute in one place: category, metal, purity, price range, diamond carat weight with quick-select ranges, and gross or net weight. Applied filters show as removable chips, with Clear all and Reset.

catalogue link

Everything about a piece, in one view

The product view shows the photos with a carousel, barcode, metal and purity, gross and net weight, HUID, size, stones and the price inclusive of taxes. The customer picks how they want the item, adds their name and phone, and submits a request.

catalogue link

A wishlist that is ready to request

Saved pieces sit in a wishlist with category tabs, quantity controls and an item count. Request all sends the whole list in one go. Products without photos still show clearly with an Image unavailable state.

Designed for every screen.

Customers open the link on whatever they have. The catalogue, product view and wishlist are designed for web, laptop and mobile.
