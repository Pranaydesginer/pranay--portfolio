Questions people ask me.

What do you do, exactly?

I'm a Product Designer at Cixcent Technologies, where I design B2B SaaS products: a jewellery retail ERP, customer and store mobile apps, and fintech tools. I work end to end, from gathering requirements and mapping user flows to wireframes, prototypes and high-fidelity UI.

Where are you based?

Hyderabad, India. I'm open to full-time roles and freelance projects.

How do you work with product managers and developers?

I work closely with product managers, product owners and stakeholders from the start, during requirement gathering. I also work with customer-facing teams to refine the raw requirements collected from clients, then turn them into user flows that solve real user problems. Once the direction is agreed, I build reusable Figma components and prepare clear handoff specs for developers in Jira, working in Agile/Scrum sprints.

Do you use AI in your work?

Yes, every day. I use Claude for interaction design and Lovable to bring ideas to life faster, alongside Figma AI, ChatGPT and Perplexity for research and exploration. AI speeds up my process, but the design decisions stay human-centred.

What roles are you looking for?

Product Designer, UX Designer and User Researcher roles, especially in B2B SaaS, enterprise products and fintech. I'm also open to freelance work.

How can I reach you?

Email me at pranaydesigner03@gmail.com, or connect with me on LinkedIn. You can also see more of my work on Behance.
