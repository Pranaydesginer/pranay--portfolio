Case study · Jewellery retail ERP

Digital Savings.

A redeem flow for customers’ digital gold and silver savings: from request, to review, to an issued redemption on record.

jewellery admin · digital savings

The idea.

save in grams, redeem with ease.

Overview

Customers save in gold and silver digitally, through the My Jeweller app or at the store, and each payment is recorded in the ERP. I designed the new redeem flow: the customer sends a redeem request, it lands in the ERP, and within the admin's time limit the customer visits the store to redeem.

What the store team can do

  • Search redeem requests by customer, request ID, group and date range.
  • Open a request to see metal, grams, redeem rate and eligible amount.
  • Check the customer’s gold summary: purchased, redeemed, available and requested.
  • See each request as Approved & Pending, Completed, Rejected or Expired, with who processed it and why.
  • Track every issued redemption in Issue Redeem History.

The problem.

easy to save, no way to redeem.

Customers could already buy digital savings through the My Jeweller app or at the store, and every payment was recorded as a transaction in the ERP. But there was no proper flow to redeem those savings.

01

No redeem flow

Customers could save, but had no way to ask to redeem their gold or silver.

02

Nothing reached the store

There was no request in the ERP for the store team to review, approve or issue.

03

No timeline or status

No time limit to visit the store, and no clear status for a request.

Before

  • Customers could buy digital savings, but not redeem them.
  • Only purchases were recorded in the ERP.
  • No status or time limit for redeeming.

After

  • Customers send a redeem request, which lands in the ERP.
  • The admin sets a time limit to visit the store and redeem.
  • Redeem is issued in applicable payment modes, such as fine gold or amount.
  • Clear statuses: Approved & Pending, Completed, Rejected or Expired, with reasons.

The redeem user flow.

from the app to the counter.

  1. 01 Customer · My Jeweller app

    Request to redeem

    In Redeem Gold, the customer sees the live sell rate, their available savings, what is available to withdraw and what is locked. They enter grams within the min–max limit, choose a redemption mode such as In-store pickup, and continue.

  2. 02 Store · ERP

    The request lands in the ERP

    It appears in the Redeem Request List. Staff search by customer, request ID, group or date, and open a request to see the metal, grams, redeem rate, eligible amount and the customer’s gold summary.

  3. 03 Store · ERP

    Approve and set the redeem window

    The admin approves and sets the dates the customer can redeem between. The request shows as Approved & Pending, with who approved it and when, until the customer visits. Before the start date redeeming is unavailable; after the end date it expires automatically.

  4. 04 Store visit

    Completed at the store

    The customer visits within the redeem window and the store redeems it as fine gold or amount. The panel shows who processed it, comments for the customer and the store, and the issue payment details.

  5. 05 Store · ERP

    Or rejected, with a reason

    The admin can reject a request. The panel shows who rejected it, when, and the reason, so the store and the customer both know what happened.

  6. 06 Store · ERP

    Or expired automatically

    If the customer does not redeem before the redeem window ends, the request becomes Expired on its own and is marked in red.

  7. 07 Store · ERP

    Every redemption on record

    Issue Redeem History keeps each redemption: date and time, redeem ID, customer, group, grams, rate per gram, amount and who redeemed it.

What I delivered.

  • Redeem Request ListSearchable list of requests by customer, ID, group and date range.
  • Request detail panelRequest information and the customer’s gold summary in one side panel.
  • Status statesApproved & Pending, Completed, Rejected and Expired, each with its own details.
